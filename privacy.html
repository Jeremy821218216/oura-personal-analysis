Personal Oura Analysis

Privacy policy

Prepared October 20, 2026. This policy describes the intended operation of a private application used solely by its operator to analyze their own Oura account.

Data and purpose

With the owner's authorization, the application retrieves available Oura account information, sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, workouts, tags, sessions, blood oxygen, ring configuration, stress, and heart-health records. Actual access depends on permissions and data availability. The purpose is personal historical analysis and trend reporting.

Storage and processing

Downloaded records and reports are stored in the owner's chosen workspace. This workspace is currently in an iCloud Drive folder and may sync to the owner's Apple account and devices according to their settings. Credentials are to be kept separately from the public website and source repository.

If the owner asks an assistant such as Codex to analyze selected records, those records may be processed by that assistant's service under the owner's account settings and the service's applicable policies. Such analysis is distinct from publishing data on this website.

Sharing

The application is not designed to sell data, serve advertising, or train machine-learning models. Health data and credentials are not intended for publication in the GitHub repository or on this website. The operator does not offer access to other people's Oura accounts.

Retention and control

The owner retains downloads and reports until they choose to delete them. They may stop collection by disabling the downloader or revoking the application's Oura authorization. Revocation does not itself remove previously downloaded files. Deleting those files may also require removing synchronized copies, backups, and assistant uploads using the relevant services' controls.

This public website

The pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or application login. GitHub Pages, if used to host them, may process connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Operator and contact

The operator is the sole authorized user. Oura can contact the operator using the contact email supplied in the application's developer registration. The application does not currently accept other users.
